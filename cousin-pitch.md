# Text to send your cousin (draft — rewritten from Benny's 11/20 notes)

**Subject:** Nashville thing I'm building — want in?

Cuz,

You know how Broadway works. Somebody's standing on the corner at 9pm with no idea which door to walk
into, and the rooms booking real country don't get found because they're not the loudest ones.

I'm building the thing that answers it. One page, no app: scan a code, pick the night, pick what you
want to hear, get a short list of rooms within walking distance — described honestly, with a
"skip the party bars" filter. That filter alone is why people will use it.

The part nobody else has is the distribution. It's not just flyers. Magnets for locals — those live on
fridges and get used every weekend. Flyers and posters in the bars, in windows, at the doorways. Counter
cards and window magnets in the boot and product stores where the tourists already are. And the bands
hand it out themselves — every act I list gets cards and magnets with their own show on the front, so it
travels to their next gig. Sponsoring bars keep all three stocked.

It doesn't turn into an ad wall, either. Sponsors get a line and a logo, not the page. A party bar is
listed as a party bar whether they pay or not — that's the only reason the recommendations are worth
anything, and it's why people keep picking it up.

Money side: featured nights $75/week, partner rooms $450/month, print sponsorship $250 per 1,000. Print
is cheap and the audience is already standing downtown.

I'm not asking for a check to sit in a drawer. I want a partner who'll push it with me. Tell me what
you'd want your end to look like and let's talk this week — deck attached, it's a two-page read.

Ben
