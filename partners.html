What the directory actually is

A walkable guide to Lower Broadway, band-first. Not a list of bars — a list of who is on stage right now, with the stage or floor named, the set time, the cover, the age rule, and the walk. People narrow it by mood (two-stepping, classic country, rowdy singalong, somewhere they can talk), by age range, and by what they want to spend.

The person using it

  • Standing on Broadway, phone in hand, deciding right now
  • Tourists here for two days; locals out on a Tuesday
  • Bachelorette groups and couples who want different rooms

How it reaches them

  • Magnets — take-home, locals keep them, repeat use
  • Flyers and posters — doors, windows, inside the rooms
  • Counter cards in boot and product stores
  • The bands hand them out to their own crowd
