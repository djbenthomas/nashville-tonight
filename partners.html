Nashville Tonight

The people are already downtown.
We just tell them where to go.

A hand-curated directory of Nashville's live music rooms — by music, by night, by the kind of night someone actually wants. Distributed on flyers with a QR code, kept current weekly.

What it is, in plain English

Somebody standing on Broadway at 9pm with a phone has no good way to know which room has real country and which one has a rock band with a cowboy hat. Google gives them a list of bars. We give them an answer.

Scan the flyer → pick the night → pick what you want to hear → get a short list of rooms, in walking distance, described honestly.

Who's looking at it

The crowd

  • Locals and visitors who came for the music, not the shots
  • Skews 30+, spends money, stays for a full set
  • Planning their night on a phone, in the street, right now

The distribution

  • Magnets — the core piece. Take-home, stays on a local's fridge, gets used every weekend
  • Flyers and posters — bar doors, honky tonk doorways, windows, inside sponsoring bars
  • Counter cards in boot and product stores — where the tourists already are, buying the boots
  • Handed out by the bands themselves — every act playing the rooms we list gets magnets and cards for their own crowd, so it travels to the next gig
  • Sponsoring bars keep all three stocked — flyers at the door, posters inside, magnets on the bar
  • Every batch has its own QR code, so scans are attributable to a location and a distributor
  • Weekly refresh — the listings are checked, not scraped once and abandoned

Sponsors get a line and a logo — not the page. The listings stay editorial, because that's the only reason anyone picks it up.

What this is not

Not pay-for-praise

  • Nobody buys a good description. A party bar is listed as a party bar whether they pay or not.
  • No fake reviews, no paid rankings presented as editorial picks.

Not a listing farm

  • 23 rooms, hand-checked. It stays small enough to be right.
  • Every card shows a "last verified" date and a link to report an error.
